## hard-scifi-constraints

- Type: internal physics constraint note / speculative fiction boundary
- Date: 2026-05-12
- Used by: Chapter 0, Chapter 7, glossary terms `causal-echo`, `low-bandwidth-causality`, `mirror-trace-protocol`
- Notes: The draft intentionally avoids material time travel, supernatural messages, or clear high-bandwidth communication from the future. This is a worldbuilding rule, not a claim that current physics has demonstrated backward communication.
